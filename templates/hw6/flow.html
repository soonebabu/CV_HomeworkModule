Part 1 - Optical flow as a video

Upload one of your videos and choose a 30 s window with motion in it. Every consecutive pair of frames goes through dense Farneback optical flow. The result is a video with the flow drawn two ways: arrows on the frame, and a colour-coded field where hue is the direction (see the wheel in the corner) and brightness is the speed. The same pass measures how much, where and which way things move, and splits camera motion from object motion. See the theory for why those numbers mean what they mean.

Working... a 30 s clip takes a minute or two. Leave this tab open.
