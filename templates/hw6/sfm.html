Part 3 - Structure from motion with four views of a flat object

Upload four photos of the same flat object, taken from four different positions. On the next page you click the same points on the object (its corners, plus a few points along the edges) in the same order in every photo. From those clicks alone, plus the camera intrinsics, the page recovers:

  • where each camera was and how it was rotated;
  • the 3-D position of every clicked point;
  • the object's plane, and its boundary in real units, which is then checked against your ruler measurements.

How it works is on the theory page.

Click the same points in all four views

Pick a starting corner and go around the object's boundary in one direction, clicking the corners and a few clear points along the edges - for example the 4 corners plus the 4 edge midpoints, or a notch or logo edge. Use the same points in the same order in every view. At least 4 points are needed and 6-10 is better, since the extra points give the least-squares steps something to average over. The loupe on the right shows the pixels under the cursor, magnified.


Scale

Images alone only give shape up to scale. Give one real distance between two of your points (measured with a ruler).
